Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains the information TOONITE processes when you use the app and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

TOONITE may process account information such as your email address, username, name, college, date of birth, age, college year, bio and profile photo. We also process functions, Stories, captions, saved functions, interests, follows, blocks, reports and other content or actions you choose to submit.

Location

When you give permission, TOONITE may use device location to show nearby functions, distance and directions-related features. You can deny or change location permission in your device settings. Function hosts may separately choose to post an event address.

Photos and videos

If you grant photo-library permission, TOONITE can access the photos or videos you select for profile pictures and Stories. TOONITE does not need access to media you do not choose to upload. Video Stories may be unavailable while TOONITE develops additional moderation safeguards.

Content moderation

TOONITE may process uploaded images using automated moderation services to help detect nudity, sexual content and other material that may violate TOONITE rules. TOONITE currently uses Sightengine for image-content moderation.

Technical information

The service and its infrastructure may process device, network, log, crash, authentication, security and usage information needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot TOONITE.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide accounts and profiles, display functions and Stories, enable follows, saves and interests, personalize relevant app experiences, prevent abuse, investigate reports, maintain security, troubleshoot problems and improve TOONITE.

Public information

Profiles, public Stories and posted functions are designed to be visible to other TOONITE users. Do not post information you do not want other users to see. Your email and password are not intended to be public profile information.

Service providers

TOONITE uses service providers to operate app infrastructure. Supabase is currently used for authentication, database and storage functions. Sightengine is currently used for image-content moderation. Mapping, geocoding, device-platform and app-distribution providers may also process information necessary to provide their services.

Blocking and reports

When you block someone, TOONITE stores the block relationship so the app can reduce interactions and visibility. When you submit a report, TOONITE stores the report, the reported account or function, your account identifier, the selected reason, optional details, status and submission time so it can be reviewed.

Retention

TOONITE keeps information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent abuse and protect users. Stories are designed to stop appearing after 24 hours, although deletion from every backup or storage system may not be instantaneous.

Account deletion

You can request permanent account deletion from Settings. TOONITE will delete the account and associated database information controlled by the deletion process. Limited records may remain where required or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, backup integrity or dispute handling.

Your choices

You can edit profile information, remove your own Stories or functions, manage device permissions, block users, report content and delete your account. You can also sign out at any time.

Age requirement

TOONITE is intended only for adults age 18 and older. TOONITE asks for a date of birth to enforce this requirement. Users under 18 are not permitted to create or use a TOONITE account.

Security

TOONITE uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This policy may change as TOONITE adds features or service providers. Material updates should be communicated through the app or another reasonable method, with a new effective date.

Contact

Privacy and safety questions can be submitted through TOONITE's in-app support and reporting tools.
